Controls (操作)

  • Walk with WASD or arrow keys (WASD 或方向键移动). Hold Shift to run (按住 Shift 跑步).
  • Drag to look around (拖动旋转镜头). Scroll to zoom (滚轮缩放).
  • On a phone, use the joystick (手机用左下摇杆移动).
  • Press E or Join near a group, then pick a chat and its level (靠近人群按 E 或 Join，再选择对话和难度). If someone is still talking, they finish the line, then welcome you. One person speaks at a time (别人说完再欢迎你。一次只有一个人说话).
  • Press T or Go to open the teleport menu (按 T 或 Go 打开传送). Glowing rings mark the portals. A short fade carries you to the place you pick (发光的环是传送门，画面淡出后再出现在目的地).
  • In the Playhouse, press E at a seat to sit and watch the show on the board (在剧场座位上按 E，坐下看正在演出的短剧). At the end you can take a role (结束时可以选一个角色).
  • When it is your turn, a soft glow says “Your turn — take your time.” There is no countdown. After a quiet moment someone may say “Take your time.” (轮到你时会轻轻提示，没有倒计时。安静一会儿后，可能会有人说“慢慢来”。)
  • Press F or Talk for a 1-on-1 chat (按 F 或 Talk 一对一聊天).
  • Near a 🎭 Skit spot, press G (or the Skit button) to watch a short scene, then take a role (靠近小剧场标记按 G，先看再扮演). Any real try counts. There is no fail.
  • Reply with a choice, the text box, or hold Space / the mic button (点选项、打字，或按住空格/麦克风说话).
  • Hint shows the first words. Retry lets you say the line again (Hint 显示开头几个词，Retry 再讲一次).
  • The gear opens settings: voice, speed, subtitles, 中文翻译, and level (齿轮打开设置).
  • Press 1 wave, 2 nod, 3 jump (1 挥手, 2 点头, 3 跳跃).
  • Press Esc or Leave to step away (Esc 或 Leave 离开对话). Esc also closes the chooser, notebook, practice, and settings.
  • At the end of a chat, press Practice on a phrase for Listen & Repeat (对话结束后可以跟读练习).
  • Press N or the book button for your phrase notebook (按 N 或笔记本按钮). Tap ☆ on a line to save it (点 ☆ 保存一句).
  • Daily missions sit on the left (左侧是每日任务). A new local day picks three new ones.
  • The gear includes an Avatar panel: name, body color, and hair (齿轮里的形象可以改名字、衣服颜色和发型).
  • After this card, three short tips show once (关闭说明后会有三步提示，只出现一次). Skip remembers that choice.
  • Press H or ? to open or close this list (按 H 或 ? 打开或关闭说明). Esc or Got it also closes it (Esc 或知道了也会关闭).
